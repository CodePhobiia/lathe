Phase 11 reference / moodboard fixtures. Each subdirectory is named after a hero asset (e.g. `industrial_crate/`) and carries up to four small, deterministic JSON files (`mood.json`, `silhouette.json`, `materials.json`, `negative.json`) consumed by `lathe.asset_quality.reference_board.load_reference_board`. These fixtures are an internal Phase 11 convention loaded by the executor and published to `runtime["_reference_board"]`; they are NOT part of the public `.lth` language surface (Phase 13 is the only phase allowed to expose this surface to `.lth`). Phase 14 will introduce richer golden examples on top of this layout.
